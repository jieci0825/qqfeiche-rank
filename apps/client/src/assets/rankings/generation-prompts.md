# 国服排行默认图片

工具：内置 `image_gen.imagegen`，`transparent_background: false`。两张图片均为新生成的通用演示素材，用作赛车和宠物图片缺失时的回退图。

文件：`default-car.png`、`default-pet.png`。

## 赛车

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: square default racing car avatar for a dark Chinese ink-and-gold racing leaderboard website.
Primary request: one generic futuristic racing sports car, shown completely in a front three-quarter view, centered, instantly recognizable at 48 pixels.
Style/medium: polished restrained 3D game inventory illustration, simple strong silhouette, ivory silver body, muted champagne gold highlights, dark wheels and dark tinted windshield.
Scene/backdrop: full-bleed charcoal dark gray subtle gradient, very faint diagonal speed streaks, no floor or scenery, no decorative frame.
Composition/framing: square 1:1, generous 14% safe padding around the whole car; car fills the central 72%, readable under a circular avatar crop.
Lighting/mood: soft studio lighting with a delicate warm rim light, subdued premium aesthetic.
Constraints: exactly one car; no people, text, logos, letters, badges, watermark, UI, or numbers.
```

## 宠物

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: square default pet avatar for a dark Chinese ink-and-gold racing leaderboard website.
Primary request: one friendly small fantasy fox companion with two pointed ears, a rounded ivory face and body, tiny paws, a fluffy tail, and simple expressive dark eyes; centered seated front three-quarter pose.
Style/medium: polished restrained 3D game inventory illustration, cute compact chibi proportions and strong simple silhouette, ivory silver fur with muted champagne gold ear tips and tail tip.
Scene/backdrop: full-bleed charcoal dark gray subtle gradient, very faint diagonal streaks, no floor or scenery, no decorative frame.
Composition/framing: square 1:1, generous 14% safe padding around the whole pet, fills the central 72%, readable at 48 pixels and under a circular avatar crop.
Lighting/mood: soft studio lighting with a delicate warm rim light, subdued premium aesthetic.
Constraints: exactly one pet; no people, cars, text, logos, letters, badges, watermark, UI, or numbers.
```
